What each rubric measures

Relevance

Does the Pro opening argue for the topic and stay on-topic? Penalizes wrong-side, off-topic, or degenerate openings.

Rebuttal Engagement

Does the Con rebuttal engage ≥1 specific point Pro actually made, rather than a generic anti-topic speech?

Moderator Soundness

Does the moderator's decision follow from the closing arguments and name a clear, valid winner?
